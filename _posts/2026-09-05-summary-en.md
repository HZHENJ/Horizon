## [Declarative Attention Lets Language Models Control Their Own Attention](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1w7sgf3/language_models_can_control_their_own_attention_r/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A new paper introduces Declarative Attention (DA), a protocol that lets language models declare where to attend within their chain-of-thought using <global>, <focus>, and <local> modes. In zero-shot evaluation on 15 long-context tasks, DA on Gemma-4-31B and Qwen-3.6-27B reduced total attended decoding tokens by 52.0% and 31.1% respectively, with accuracy drops of 1.27 and 2.75 percentage points. Unlike extrinsic proxy scoring that still incurs O(N) cost per step, DA leverages the model's own knowledge to skip irrelevant KV cache reads, making long-context inference intrinsically cheaper. It also opens a new axis for sparse attention beyond fixed or learned patterns. The three generation modes are <global> (full context), <focus> (specific region), and <local> (recent output only); the inference engine parses these declarations like tool calls to skip most KV cache reads. The method was tested zero-shot across 15 long-context tasks, and the accuracy penalty was smaller for larger models.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/eigenlaplace · Sep 5, 06:07

**Impact**: In the short term, off-the-shelf long-context models can cut decoding-time KV cache scanning without retraining, which directly reduces serving cost for tasks like long conversations or document QA. Over time, inference engines may adopt declaration-style interfaces and training-based methods could further push accuracy-efficiency tradeoffs, influencing how long-context LLMs are deployed in production.

**Background**: Transformer-based language models typically store key and value vectors from previous tokens in a KV cache to reuse during autoregressive inference. For long-context inputs, global attention must read that entire cache for every newly generated token, which becomes a computational bottleneck. Chain-of-thought prompting asks models to produce intermediate reasoning steps before the final answer, and Declarative Attention inserts declarations within those steps to indicate attention regions.
